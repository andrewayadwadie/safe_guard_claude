FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is my child's data private?

Yes. The text and image safety checks run with on-device machine learning, so the content being analysed stays on the device rather than being sent to a server for scanning.

Does it need root access?

No. Haris works through standard Android permissions and does not require rooting the device.

Which devices are supported?

Haris is an Android app. Support details will be listed on the Google Play listing at launch.

Is it free?

Pricing will be confirmed on the store listing. The app is still preparing for launch.

How do I remove it?

A parent can unpair and uninstall from the parent device. Tamper protection means the child can't quietly disable it without the parent being alerted.

Can I choose what gets flagged?

Yes. Custom word lists let you define your own watch terms, and alerts are ranked by severity so you focus on what matters.
